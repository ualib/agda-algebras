# Milestone 11 reading map: commutator theory

Milestone 11 builds commutator theory from the term condition up.  This
note maps the two source books onto the milestone's issues, records the
decisions the plan entry makes, and says what is out of scope.  Read it
with the Milestone 11 entry in [`GITHUB_PROJECT.md`](../GITHUB_PROJECT.md).

## Sources

+  Ralph Freese and Ralph McKenzie, *Commutator Theory for Congruence
   Modular Varieties*, London Mathematical Society Lecture Note Series
   125, Cambridge University Press, 1987.  Cited below as FM.
+  Keith A. Kearnes and Emil W. Kiss, *The Shape of Congruence Lattices*,
   Memoirs of the American Mathematical Society 222, no. 1046, 2013.
   Cited below as KK.
+  Keith A. Kearnes and Ágnes Szendrei, The relationship between two
   commutators, International Journal of Algebra and Computation 8 (1998),
   497 to 531, for the theorems KK cite from it.
+  William DeMeo, Ralph Freese, and Matthew Valeriote, Polynomial-time
   tests for difference terms in idempotent varieties, International
   Journal of Algebra and Computation 29 (2019), for M11-12.

## Decisions

+  **Tolerances are the base notion**, as in KK, and congruences are the
   transitive case.  FM state the theory for congruences; KK's Theorem
   2.19 (2) brings a tolerance's statements back to its generated
   congruence, so nothing is lost and the Chapter 3 results on abelian
   tolerances become statable.
+  **One matrix definition is canonical**.  M(S, T) by term operations
   (FM Definition 3.2) and as the subalgebra of 𝑨⁴ generated by the
   constant-row and constant-column matrices (FM Proposition 3.3 (1)) are
   proved equivalent in M11-1, and the one that makes the closure
   arguments structural is the definition.
+  **The commutator is a least element of the congruence lattice**, taken
   in the complete lattice of M6-1 at its level conventions, so that
   [S, T] and (δ : T) are meets and joins there rather than new inductive
   definitions.
+  **Modularity enters as Day terms**, through the existing interpretation
   `Th-Day n ≼ ℰ` and Day's theorem in `Setoid.Varieties.Maltsev.Modularity`;
   Gumm terms (M11-6) are added as a second theory and proved equivalent.
+  **Difference terms are records, not theories**: FM Theorem 5.5 (ii) and
   KK Definition 6.1 mention the commutator, so a difference term is a
   term with proof obligations over a class of algebras, not an
   equational Mal'tsev condition.
+  **Theorems whose proofs leave the milestone are hypothesis records**:
   KK Theorem 6.2 and Kearnes and Szendrei's Theorem 4.8 (M11-10), the
   hard direction of KK Theorem 8.1 (M11-11), and the tame-congruence-theory
   characterization behind the difference-term test (M11-12).  Each is a
   frozen statement with its source and theorem number, discharged when a
   proof lands.

## Order

The general theory (M11-1, M11-2) comes first and needs only M6-1.  The
modular chapters (M11-4 to M11-8) and the group comparison (M11-3) can
proceed in parallel after it.  The Kearnes and Kiss chapters (M11-9 to
M11-11) need only the general theory, and M11-12 waits for M11-10.

## Out of scope

+  Tame congruence theory (Hobby and McKenzie): its type-omitting theorems
   enter as hypothesis records where a result needs one.
+  FM Chapters 10 to 15 (structure and representation in modular
   varieties, joins and products of modular varieties, strictly simple
   algebras, Mal'cev conditions for lattice equations, the finite basis
   result, pure lattice congruence identities), beyond what M11-8 states.
+  KK Chapters 4, 5, 7, and 9 (meet continuous congruence identities,
   rectangulation, ordinary congruence identities, residually small
   varieties), except where M11-11 cites them.
