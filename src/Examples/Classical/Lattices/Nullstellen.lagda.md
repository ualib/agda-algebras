### Worked example: the closure is larger than the generated congruence

This is the [Examples.Classical.Lattices.Nullstellen][] module of the [Agda Universal Algebra Library][].

McNulty's free-lattice Nullstellensatz problem asks for the closure operator that
sends a set `Σ` of equations over `FL(n)` to the set of equations holding under
every solution of `Σ` ([Classical.Structures.Lattice.Free.Closure][]).  The
closure always contains the congruence `Σ` generates; this module proves, for one
equation over `FL(3)`, that it can be strictly larger.  Let `x`, `y`, `z` be the
generators, `Σ` the single equation `x ∧ y ≈ x ∧ z`, and `e` the equation
`x ∧ y ≈ x ∧ (y ∨ z)`.

+  **`e` is in the closure of `Σ`**.  A solution of `Σ` is a triple `u, v, w` of
   elements of `FL(3)` with `u ∧ v = u ∧ w`, and since `FL(3)` is meet
   semidistributive ([Freese, Ježek, and Nation (1995)][], Theorem 1.21), every
   such triple satisfies `u ∧ v = u ∧ (v ∨ w)`.
+  **`e` is not in the congruence `Σ` generates**.  The diamond `M₃` of
   [Examples.Classical.Lattices.M3][] is generated by its three atoms, and the
   homomorphism `FL(3) → M₃` sending `x`, `y`, `z` to the atoms `a`, `b`, `c`
   sends both sides of `Σ` to the bottom element `⊥` and the two sides of `e` to
   `⊥` and `a`.  Its kernel is a congruence containing `Σ` and not `e`.

So meet semidistributivity, a law that fails in `M₃`, is an entry of the closure
operator that congruence generation does not see; dually for join
semidistributivity.  These are the first data of McNulty's problem.

#### The equations

The generators of `FL(3)` are `ℊ 0F`, `ℊ 1F`, and `ℊ 2F`.  `Σ₀`{.AgdaFunction} is
the list holding the one equation `x ∧ y ≈ x ∧ z`, and `e₀`{.AgdaFunction} the
equation `x ∧ y ≈ x ∧ (y ∨ z)`.

```agda
x y z : LatTerm (Fin 3)
x = ℊ 0F
y = ℊ 1F
z = ℊ 2F

Σ₀ : List (LatTerm (Fin 3) × LatTerm (Fin 3))
Σ₀ = (x ∧̇ y , x ∧̇ z) ∷ []

e₀ : LatTerm (Fin 3) × LatTerm (Fin 3)
e₀ = x ∧̇ y , x ∧̇ (y ∨̇ z)
```

#### In the closure

A solution `σ` of `Σ₀`{.AgdaFunction} equates `σ x ∧ σ y` and `σ x ∧ σ z`, and
meet semidistributivity of `FL(3)` (`FL-isMeetSemidistributive`{.AgdaFunction} of
[Classical.Structures.Lattice.Free.Semidistributive][], at the decidable equality
of `Fin 3`) turns that equation into `σ x ∧ σ y = σ x ∧ (σ y ∨ σ z)`, which is
`e₀`{.AgdaFunction} under `σ`.

```agda
e₀∈closure : InClosure Σ₀ e₀
e₀∈closure σ sol = FL-isMeetSemidistributive _≟_ (σ 0F) (σ 1F) (σ 2F) (lookup sol (here refl))
```

#### Not in the generated congruence

The assignment `atoms`{.AgdaFunction} sends `x`, `y`, `z` to the atoms of `M₃`.
Under it both sides of the equation of `Σ₀`{.AgdaFunction} evaluate to the bottom
element, by the operation tables of `M₃`, and the two sides of
`e₀`{.AgdaFunction} evaluate to the bottom element and the atom `a`, which are
different elements of `Fin 5`.  `Cg-separated`{.AgdaFunction} of
[Classical.Structures.Lattice.Free.Closure][] turns this into the statement that
`e₀`{.AgdaFunction} lies outside the congruence `Σ₀`{.AgdaFunction} generates.

```agda
atoms : Fin 3 → Fin 5
atoms 0F = a
atoms 1F = b
atoms 2F = c

e₀∉Cg : ¬ InCg Σ₀ e₀
e₀∉Cg = Cg-separated M₃ atoms (refl ∷ []) λ ()
```

#### The closure is strictly larger

Together the two facts say that the closure of `Σ₀`{.AgdaFunction} contains an
equation that the congruence it generates does not (`closure≠Cg`{.AgdaFunction}).

```agda
closure≠Cg : InClosure Σ₀ e₀ × ¬ InCg Σ₀ e₀
closure≠Cg = e₀∈closure , e₀∉Cg
```
