### Worked example: the diamond `M₃`

This is the [Examples.Classical.Lattices.M3][] module of the [Agda Universal Algebra Library][].

The lattice `M₃` has a bottom `⊥`, a top `⊤`, and three atoms `a`, `b`, `c`
between them, any two of which meet to `⊥` and join to `⊤`.  It is the smallest
modular lattice that is not distributive, and it separates the conditions of
[Classical.Properties.Lattice.Semidistributive][]: it satisfies Whitman's
condition (W) but neither semidistributive law.

+  **(W) holds**.  If `s₁` and `s₂` are comparable, their meet is one of them, so
   a meet below `t₁ ∨ t₂` has a meetand below it.  Otherwise their meet is `⊥`,
   which lies below `t₁`.
+  **SD∨ fails**.  `a ∨ b = ⊤ = a ∨ c`, yet `a ∨ (b ∧ c) = a ∨ ⊥ = a`.
+  **SD∧ fails**, dually.  `a ∧ b = ⊥ = a ∧ c`, yet `a ∧ (b ∨ c) = a ∧ ⊤ = a`.

So `M₃` is not a sublattice of any free lattice, although it satisfies (W).
These verdicts are not proved here by hand.  The lattice is given by its two
operation tables through [Classical.Structures.Lattice.Table][], and each verdict
is the value of a decision procedure of
[Classical.Properties.Lattice.Semidistributive][], extracted by `from-yes` or
`from-no`; a wrong table, or a wrong procedure, would make the module fail to
type-check.  The three equations of the SD∨ failure above, and their duals, are
checked separately by evaluation, as an illustration of what the procedure found.

#### The tables

The carrier is `Fin 5`, with `⊥`, `a`, `b`, `c`, `⊤` numbered `0` to `4`.

| `∧` | ⊥ | a | b | c | ⊤ |   | `∨` | ⊥ | a | b | c | ⊤ |
|-----|---|---|---|---|---|---|-----|---|---|---|---|---|
| ⊥   | ⊥ | ⊥ | ⊥ | ⊥ | ⊥ |   | ⊥   | ⊥ | a | b | c | ⊤ |
| a   | ⊥ | a | ⊥ | ⊥ | a |   | a   | a | a | ⊤ | ⊤ | ⊤ |
| b   | ⊥ | ⊥ | b | ⊥ | b |   | b   | b | ⊤ | b | ⊤ | ⊤ |
| c   | ⊥ | ⊥ | ⊥ | c | c |   | c   | c | ⊤ | ⊤ | c | ⊤ |
| ⊤   | ⊥ | a | b | c | ⊤ |   | ⊤   | ⊤ | ⊤ | ⊤ | ⊤ | ⊤ |

```agda
infixl 7 _∧_ _∨_

⊥ a b c ⊤ : Fin 5
⊥ = 0F
a = 1F
b = 2F
c = 3F
⊤ = 4F

∧-table : Table 5
∧-table = (0F ∷ 0F ∷ 0F ∷ 0F ∷ 0F ∷ [])
        ∷ (0F ∷ 1F ∷ 0F ∷ 0F ∷ 1F ∷ [])
        ∷ (0F ∷ 0F ∷ 2F ∷ 0F ∷ 2F ∷ [])
        ∷ (0F ∷ 0F ∷ 0F ∷ 3F ∷ 3F ∷ [])
        ∷ (0F ∷ 1F ∷ 2F ∷ 3F ∷ 4F ∷ [])
        ∷ []

∨-table : Table 5
∨-table = (0F ∷ 1F ∷ 2F ∷ 3F ∷ 4F ∷ [])
        ∷ (1F ∷ 1F ∷ 4F ∷ 4F ∷ 4F ∷ [])
        ∷ (2F ∷ 4F ∷ 2F ∷ 4F ∷ 4F ∷ [])
        ∷ (3F ∷ 4F ∷ 4F ∷ 3F ∷ 4F ∷ [])
        ∷ (4F ∷ 4F ∷ 4F ∷ 4F ∷ 4F ∷ [])
        ∷ []

_∧_ _∨_ : Fin 5 → Fin 5 → Fin 5
_∧_ = ⟦ ∧-table ⟧
_∨_ = ⟦ ∨-table ⟧
```

#### The lattice

The eight lattice laws of the two tables are decided together, and
`from-yes`{.AgdaFunction} of the decision is their proof (`M₃-laws`{.AgdaFunction});
`M₃`{.AgdaFunction} is the lattice they make, and `M₃-finite`{.AgdaFunction} its
enumeration by the identity on `Fin 5`.

```agda
M₃-laws : LatticeTableLaws _∧_ _∨_
M₃-laws = from-yes (latticeTableLaws? _∧_ _∨_)

M₃ : Lattice 0ℓ 0ℓ
M₃ = tableLattice _∧_ _∨_ M₃-laws

M₃-finite : FiniteAlgebra (proj₁ M₃)
M₃-finite = tableLattice-FiniteAlgebra _∧_ _∨_ M₃-laws
```

#### The verdicts

Each verdict is a decision procedure evaluated on `M₃`{.AgdaFunction}: `from-yes`
when it answers yes, `from-no` when it answers no.  The sweep for (W) visits all
`5⁴ = 625` quadruples, and each semidistributive law `5³ = 125` triples.

```agda
M₃-satisfiesW : SatisfiesW M₃
M₃-satisfiesW = from-yes (satisfiesW? M₃ M₃-finite)

M₃-not-joinSemidistributive : ¬ IsJoinSemidistributive M₃
M₃-not-joinSemidistributive = from-no (isJoinSemidistributive? M₃ M₃-finite)

M₃-not-meetSemidistributive : ¬ IsMeetSemidistributive M₃
M₃-not-meetSemidistributive = from-no (isMeetSemidistributive? M₃ M₃-finite)
```

#### The failing instances

The instance of SD∨ that fails is `a ∨ b = ⊤ = a ∨ c` with `a ∨ (b ∧ c) = a`, and
the instance of SD∧ that fails is its dual; both hold by evaluating the tables.

```agda
M₃-SD∨-failure : (a ∨ b ≡ ⊤) × (a ∨ c ≡ ⊤) × (a ∨ (b ∧ c) ≡ a)
M₃-SD∨-failure = refl , refl , refl

M₃-SD∧-failure : (a ∧ b ≡ ⊥) × (a ∧ c ≡ ⊥) × (a ∧ (b ∨ c) ≡ a)
M₃-SD∧-failure = refl , refl , refl
```
