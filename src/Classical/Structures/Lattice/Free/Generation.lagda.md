### Free generating sets

This is the [Classical.Structures.Lattice.Free.Generation][] module of the [Agda Universal Algebra Library][].

When does a family of elements of a free lattice generate a sublattice that is
itself free, with the family as its free generating set?  [Freese, Ježek, and
Nation (1995)][], Corollary 1.13, answers: exactly when no member lies below the
join of finitely many other members, and no member lies above the meet of finitely
many other members.  For a finite family the condition quantifies over finitely
many subfamilies, and each instance is a single decision of Whitman's procedure,
so the criterion is decidable.  It is the tool by which Dean's problem, whether a
free lattice has an infinite ascending chain of sublattices isomorphic to `FL(3)`,
is studied in Freese's notes on the problem ("Notes on Dean's problem", 1997), and
the worked example [Examples.Classical.Lattices.DeansProblem][] applies it to
Freese's example.

A family is a substitution `σ` from an index set `I` to the generators `X`
([Classical.Structures.Lattice.Free.Substitution][]), and "the sublattice
generated by the family is freely generated by it" means that the homomorphism
`FL(I) → FL(X)` the substitution induces is injective: it is then an isomorphism
of `FL(I)` onto its image, which is the sublattice generated by the family.  The
module proves the following:

+  **Sufficiency** (Corollary 1.12).  If the family satisfies the condition, the
   induced homomorphism reflects Whitman's order, hence is injective.  The proof
   is the book's: the images of the generators obey, inside the sublattice they
   generate, the six rules of Whitman's procedure (Theorem 1.11), so an
   inequality between images is derived by the same rules as the inequality
   between the terms.  Rules 2, 3, and 6 hold in all of `FL(X)`; rule 1 is the
   condition for a single other member; and rules 4 and 5, that each member is
   join prime and meet prime within the sublattice, are Lemma 1.3, which extends
   primality from joins of members to joins of arbitrary elements of the
   sublattice.
+  **Necessity**.  If the induced homomorphism is injective, the condition holds,
   because the generators of `FL(I)` are join prime and meet prime.
+  **The decision procedure** (`freelyGenerates?`{.AgdaFunction}), for an index set
   that is enumerated and has decidable equality.
+  **Corollary 1.6(1)**: a free generator lies in the sublattice generated by a
   family only if it equals a member of the family.  Through an embedding this
   decides when one free sublattice properly contains another.

#### Joins and meets of finite lists

The condition of Corollary 1.13 speaks of the join of a finite set of members,
and the set may be empty, while a free lattice on infinitely many generators has
no least element.  So the statement "`b` lies below the join of the list `G`" is
a relation of its own, `b ≤⋁ G`{.AgdaFunction}, false for the empty list and
`b ≤ʷ ⋁⁺ g gs` for a list `g ∷ gs`; dually `G ⋀≤ b`{.AgdaFunction}.  A member of
a list lies below its join (`∈→≤⋁`{.AgdaFunction}); the relation is closed
downward on the left (`≤ʷ-≤⋁`{.AgdaFunction}) and under joins on the left
(`∨-≤⋁`{.AgdaFunction}); and if every member of `F` lies below the join of `G`,
anything below the join of `F` does too (`≤⋁-cover`{.AgdaFunction}); dually
for meets (`∈→⋀≤`{.AgdaFunction}, `⋀≤-cover`{.AgdaFunction}).

```agda
infix 4 _≤⋁_ _⋀≤_

_≤⋁_ : {X : Type χ} → LatTerm X → List (LatTerm X) → Type χ
b ≤⋁ []        = ⊥
b ≤⋁ (g ∷ gs)  = b ≤ʷ ⋁⁺ g gs

_⋀≤_ : {X : Type χ} → List (LatTerm X) → LatTerm X → Type χ
[] ⋀≤ b        = ⊥
(g ∷ gs) ⋀≤ b  = ⋀⁺ g gs ≤ʷ b

∈→≤⋁ : b ∈ G → b ≤⋁ G
∈→≤⋁ {G = []}     ()
∈→≤⋁ {G = _ ∷ _}  b∈ = ⋁⁺-upper b∈

≤ʷ-≤⋁ : b ≤ʷ c → c ≤⋁ G → b ≤⋁ G
≤ʷ-≤⋁ {G = []}     _ ()
≤ʷ-≤⋁ {G = _ ∷ _}  p q = ≤ʷ-trans p q

∨-≤⋁ : u ≤⋁ G → v ≤⋁ G → u ∨̇ v ≤⋁ G
∨-≤⋁ {G = []}     () _
∨-≤⋁ {G = _ ∷ _}  p q = ∨≤ p q

≤⋁-cover : b ≤⋁ F → (∀ {f} → f ∈ F → f ≤⋁ G) → b ≤⋁ G
≤⋁-cover {F = []}               ()  _
≤⋁-cover {F = _ ∷ _} {G = []}     _ h = h (here refl)
≤⋁-cover {F = _ ∷ _} {G = _ ∷ _}  p h = ≤ʷ-trans p (⋁⁺-least h)

∈→⋀≤ : b ∈ G → G ⋀≤ b
∈→⋀≤ {G = []}     ()
∈→⋀≤ {G = _ ∷ _}  b∈ = ⋀⁺-lower b∈

⋀≤-cover : F ⋀≤ b → (∀ {f} → f ∈ F → G ⋀≤ f) → G ⋀≤ b
⋀≤-cover {F = []}               ()  _
⋀≤-cover {F = _ ∷ _} {G = []}     _ h = h (here refl)
⋀≤-cover {F = _ ∷ _} {G = _ ∷ _}  p h = ≤ʷ-trans (⋀⁺-greatest h) p
```

The dual of a meet of a list is the join of the duals (`dual-⋀⁺`{.AgdaFunction}),
which is how a statement about meets of members becomes one about joins.

```agda
dual-⋀⁺ : (u : LatTerm X) (us : List (LatTerm X)) → dual (⋀⁺ u us) ≡ ⋁⁺ (dual u) (map dual us)
dual-⋀⁺ u []        = refl
dual-⋀⁺ u (w ∷ ws)  = cong (dual u ∨̇_) (dual-⋀⁺ w ws)
```

#### The condition of Corollary 1.13

A family `σ` indexed by `I` satisfies the *join condition*
(`JoinCondition`{.AgdaFunction}) if a member `σ i` lies below the join of the
members indexed by a finite list `Y` only when `i` is in `Y`, and the *meet
condition* (`MeetCondition`{.AgdaFunction}) dually.  It *freely generates*
(`FreelyGenerates`{.AgdaFunction}) if it satisfies both.  Membership is that of
indices, so two indices with equal members violate the condition, as they must:
the homomorphism from `FL(I)` then identifies two generators.  (Taking `Y` to be
the single index `j` gives the case `σ i ≤ σ j`, so the condition contains rule 1
of Whitman's procedure for the members.)

```agda
JoinCondition : {I : Type ι} {X : Type χ} → Sub I X → Type (ι ⊔ χ)
JoinCondition {I = I} σ = ∀ {i : I} {Y : List I} → σ i ≤⋁ map σ Y → i ∈ Y

MeetCondition : {I : Type ι} {X : Type χ} → Sub I X → Type (ι ⊔ χ)
MeetCondition {I = I} σ = ∀ {i : I} {Y : List I} → map σ Y ⋀≤ σ i → i ∈ Y

FreelyGenerates : {I : Type ι} {X : Type χ} → Sub I X → Type (ι ⊔ χ)
FreelyGenerates σ = JoinCondition σ × MeetCondition σ
```

#### Lemma 1.3: primality spreads through the generated sublattice

Fix an element `a` of `FL(X)`.  Say that `a` is *prime over* a predicate `P`
(`PrimeOn`{.AgdaFunction}) if, whenever `a` lies below the join of a finite list
of elements satisfying `P`, it lies below one of them.  [Freese, Ježek, and Nation
(1995)][], Lemma 1.3, says that if `a` is prime over the generators of a lattice,
it is prime over the whole lattice.  The proof grows the predicate one element at
a time and shows that primality survives adding the meet or the join of two
elements already present.  Primality is antitone in the predicate
(`PrimeOn-⊆`{.AgdaFunction}).

```agda
module _ {X : Type χ} (a : LatTerm X) where
  PrimeOn : (LatTerm X → Type ℓ) → Type (χ ⊔ ℓ)
  PrimeOn P = ∀ {F} → All P F → a ≤⋁ F → Any (a ≤ʷ_) F

  PrimeOn-⊆ : {P : LatTerm X → Type ℓ} {Q : LatTerm X → Type ℓ′}
            → (∀ {e} → Q e → P e) → PrimeOn P → PrimeOn Q
  PrimeOn-⊆ Q⊆P pr qs = pr (All.map Q⊆P qs)
```

Both steps rewrite a list `F` of elements satisfying `P` or equal to a new element
`t`.  `squash w F ps`{.AgdaFunction} replaces each occurrence of `t` in `F` by
`w`, using the proof `ps` to tell the occurrences apart; the result satisfies `P`
throughout when `w` does (`squash-All`{.AgdaFunction}).  Each member of `F`
survives the replacement or is `t`, with `w` in its place
(`squash-∈`{.AgdaFunction}), so if `t` lies below `w`, every member of `F` lies
below the join of the result (`squash-cover`{.AgdaFunction}).  And if `a` lies
below a member of the result, it lies below a member of `F`, or below `w` with
`t` a member of `F` (`squash-Any`{.AgdaFunction}).

```agda
  module _ {P : LatTerm X → Type ℓ} {t : LatTerm X} where
    squash : (w : LatTerm X) (F : List (LatTerm X)) → All (λ e → P e ⊎ e ≡ t) F
           → List (LatTerm X)
    squash w []       []             = []
    squash w (e ∷ F)  (inj₁ _ ∷ ps)  = e ∷ squash w F ps
    squash w (e ∷ F)  (inj₂ _ ∷ ps)  = w ∷ squash w F ps

    squash-All : (w : LatTerm X) → P w → (ps : All (λ e → P e ⊎ e ≡ t) F)
               → All P (squash w F ps)
    squash-All w pw []              = []
    squash-All w pw (inj₁ pe ∷ ps)  = pe ∷ squash-All w pw ps
    squash-All w pw (inj₂ _ ∷ ps)   = pw ∷ squash-All w pw ps

    squash-∈ : (w : LatTerm X) (ps : All (λ e → P e ⊎ e ≡ t) F) → b ∈ F
             → b ∈ squash w F ps ⊎ (b ≡ t × w ∈ squash w F ps)
    squash-∈ w (inj₁ _ ∷ ps)    (here refl)  = inj₁ (here refl)
    squash-∈ w (inj₂ e≡t ∷ ps)  (here refl)  = inj₂ (e≡t , here refl)
    squash-∈ w (inj₁ _ ∷ ps)    (there b∈)   = ⊎-map there (map₂ there) (squash-∈ w ps b∈)
    squash-∈ w (inj₂ _ ∷ ps)    (there b∈)   = ⊎-map there (map₂ there) (squash-∈ w ps b∈)

    squash-cover : (w : LatTerm X) → t ≤ʷ w → (ps : All (λ e → P e ⊎ e ≡ t) F) → b ∈ F
                 → b ≤⋁ squash w F ps
    squash-cover w t≤w ps b∈ =
      [ ∈→≤⋁ , (λ (b≡t , w∈) → ≤ʷ-≤⋁ (subst (_≤ʷ w) (sym b≡t) t≤w) (∈→≤⋁ w∈)) ]′
      (squash-∈ w ps b∈)

    squash-Any : (w : LatTerm X) (ps : All (λ e → P e ⊎ e ≡ t) F)
               → Any (a ≤ʷ_) (squash w F ps) → Any (a ≤ʷ_) F ⊎ (a ≤ʷ w × t ∈ F)
    squash-Any w (inj₁ _ ∷ ps)    (here p)   = inj₁ (here p)
    squash-Any w (inj₂ e≡t ∷ ps)  (here p)   = inj₂ (p , here (sym e≡t))
    squash-Any w (inj₁ _ ∷ ps)    (there r)  = ⊎-map there (map₂ there) (squash-Any w ps r)
    squash-Any w (inj₂ _ ∷ ps)    (there r)  = ⊎-map there (map₂ there) (squash-Any w ps r)
```

**Adding a meet** (`∧-step`{.AgdaFunction}).  Suppose `a` is prime over `P`, the
elements `u` and `v` satisfy `P`, and `a` lies below the join of a list `F` of
elements satisfying `P` or equal to `u ∧ v`.  Replacing `u ∧ v` by `u` only
raises the join, so primality over `P` puts `a` below a member of `F`, or below
`u`; likewise with `v`.  If both answers are `u` and `v`, then `a` lies below
`u ∧ v`, which is a member of `F`.  The two answers are combined by
`meet-of-two`{.AgdaFunction}.

```agda
  meet-of-two : Any (a ≤ʷ_) F ⊎ (a ≤ʷ u × u ∧̇ v ∈ F) → Any (a ≤ʷ_) F ⊎ (a ≤ʷ v × u ∧̇ v ∈ F)
              → Any (a ≤ʷ_) F
  meet-of-two (inj₁ q)           _                 = q
  meet-of-two (inj₂ _)           (inj₁ q)          = q
  meet-of-two (inj₂ (a≤u , m∈))  (inj₂ (a≤v , _))  = Any.map (λ { refl → ≤∧ a≤u a≤v }) m∈

  ∧-step : {P : LatTerm X → Type ℓ} → PrimeOn P → P u → P v → PrimeOn (λ e → P e ⊎ e ≡ u ∧̇ v)
  ∧-step {u = u} {v} pr pu pv ps a≤ =
    meet-of-two (squash-Any u ps (pr (squash-All u pu ps) (≤⋁-cover a≤ (squash-cover u ∧̇-lowerˡ ps))))
                (squash-Any v ps (pr (squash-All v pv ps) (≤⋁-cover a≤ (squash-cover v ∧̇-lowerʳ ps))))
```

**Adding a join** (`∨-step`{.AgdaFunction}).  Now replace `u ∨ v` in `F` by `u` in
one copy of the list and by `v` in another, and concatenate the copies.  Every
member of `F` lies below the join of the result, `u ∨ v` because both `u` and `v`
are in it, so primality over `P` puts `a` below a member of one copy: a member of
`F`, or `u` or `v`, each of which lies below `u ∨ v`, a member of `F`
(`squash-settle`{.AgdaFunction}).

```agda
  squash-settle : (a ≤ʷ w → a ≤ʷ t) → Any (a ≤ʷ_) F ⊎ (a ≤ʷ w × t ∈ F) → Any (a ≤ʷ_) F
  squash-settle f (inj₁ q)           = q
  squash-settle f (inj₂ (a≤w , t∈))  = Any.map (λ { refl → f a≤w }) t∈

  ∨-step : {P : LatTerm X → Type ℓ} → PrimeOn P → P u → P v → PrimeOn (λ e → P e ⊎ e ≡ u ∨̇ v)
  ∨-step {u = u} {v} pr pu pv {F} ps a≤ =
    [ squash-settle (λ a≤u → ≤ʷ-trans a≤u ∨̇-upperˡ) ∘ squash-Any u ps
    , squash-settle (λ a≤v → ≤ʷ-trans a≤v ∨̇-upperʳ) ∘ squash-Any v ps
    ]′ (Any.++⁻ (squash u F ps)
                (pr (All.++⁺ (squash-All u pu ps) (squash-All v pv ps)) (≤⋁-cover a≤ cover)))
    where
    both : List (LatTerm X)
    both = squash u F ps ++ squash v F ps

    cover : ∀ {f} → f ∈ F → f ≤⋁ both
    cover f∈ =
      [ (λ f∈u → ∈→≤⋁ (∈-++⁺ˡ f∈u))
      , (λ (f≡ , u∈) →
          [ (λ f∈v → ∈→≤⋁ (∈-++⁺ʳ (squash u F ps) f∈v))
          , (λ (_ , v∈) → subst (_≤⋁ both) (sym f≡)
                                (∨-≤⋁ (∈→≤⋁ (∈-++⁺ˡ u∈)) (∈→≤⋁ (∈-++⁺ʳ (squash u F ps) v∈))))
          ]′ (squash-∈ v ps f∈))
      ]′ (squash-∈ u ps f∈)
```

**The sublattice generated by a family**.  Fix a family `σ`.  The elements in play
are the members `σ j`, together with a finite list `L` of further elements
(`Pool`{.AgdaFunction}).  Adding the image `t [ σ ]` of a term `t` over `I` to the
list preserves primality of `a` (`pool-extend`{.AgdaFunction}), by induction on `t`: a
generator's image is a member already, and a meet or join is added by the two
steps above after its two sides have been added.  Primality over the members alone
is the hypothesis of the lemma, read through the indices of the members
(`pool-base`{.AgdaFunction}).  Together these give Lemma 1.3 in the form the free
generation argument uses (`sublattice-join-prime`{.AgdaFunction}): if `a` is prime
over the members, then `a` below the join of two elements of the generated
sublattice lies below one of them.

```agda
module _ {I : Type ι} {X : Type χ} (σ : Sub I X) (a : LatTerm X) where
  Pool : List (LatTerm X) → LatTerm X → Type (ι ⊔ χ)
  Pool L e = (Σ[ j ∈ I ] e ≡ σ j) ⊎ e ∈ L

  pool-extend : (t : LatTerm I) {L : List (LatTerm X)}
              → PrimeOn a (Pool L) → PrimeOn a (Pool (t [ σ ] ∷ L))
  pool-extend (ℊ j)    {L} pr = PrimeOn-⊆ a member pr
    where
    member : ∀ {e} → Pool (σ j ∷ L) e → Pool L e
    member (inj₁ g)            = inj₁ g
    member (inj₂ (here refl))  = inj₁ (j , refl)
    member (inj₂ (there e∈))   = inj₂ e∈
  pool-extend (u ∧̇ v)  {L} pr =
    PrimeOn-⊆ a split (∧-step a (pool-extend v (pool-extend u pr))
                               (inj₂ (there (here refl))) (inj₂ (here refl)))
    where
    split : ∀ {e} → Pool ((u ∧̇ v) [ σ ] ∷ L) e
          → Pool (v [ σ ] ∷ u [ σ ] ∷ L) e ⊎ e ≡ u [ σ ] ∧̇ v [ σ ]
    split (inj₁ g)            = inj₁ (inj₁ g)
    split (inj₂ (here refl))  = inj₂ refl
    split (inj₂ (there e∈))   = inj₁ (inj₂ (there (there e∈)))
  pool-extend (u ∨̇ v)  {L} pr =
    PrimeOn-⊆ a split (∨-step a (pool-extend v (pool-extend u pr))
                               (inj₂ (there (here refl))) (inj₂ (here refl)))
    where
    split : ∀ {e} → Pool ((u ∨̇ v) [ σ ] ∷ L) e
          → Pool (v [ σ ] ∷ u [ σ ] ∷ L) e ⊎ e ≡ u [ σ ] ∨̇ v [ σ ]
    split (inj₁ g)            = inj₁ (inj₁ g)
    split (inj₂ (here refl))  = inj₂ refl
    split (inj₂ (there e∈))   = inj₁ (inj₂ (there (there e∈)))
```

A list of members carries the list of their indices (`indices`{.AgdaFunction}),
whose image under `σ` is the list itself (`indices-map`{.AgdaFunction}).

```agda
  indices : (F : List (LatTerm X)) → All (Pool []) F → List I
  indices []       []                   = []
  indices (_ ∷ F)  (inj₁ (j , _) ∷ ps)  = j ∷ indices F ps
  indices (_ ∷ F)  (inj₂ () ∷ ps)

  indices-map : (F : List (LatTerm X)) (ps : All (Pool []) F) → map σ (indices F ps) ≡ F
  indices-map []       []                      = refl
  indices-map (_ ∷ F)  (inj₁ (j , refl) ∷ ps)  = cong (σ j ∷_) (indices-map F ps)
  indices-map (_ ∷ F)  (inj₂ () ∷ ps)

  pool-base : (∀ {Y : List I} → a ≤⋁ map σ Y → Any (λ j → a ≤ʷ σ j) Y) → PrimeOn a (Pool [])
  pool-base H {F} ps a≤ =
    subst (Any (a ≤ʷ_)) (indices-map F ps)
          (Any.map⁺ (H (subst (a ≤⋁_) (sym (indices-map F ps)) a≤)))

  sublattice-join-prime : (∀ {Y : List I} → a ≤⋁ map σ Y → Any (λ j → a ≤ʷ σ j) Y)
                        → (u v : LatTerm I) → a ≤ʷ u [ σ ] ∨̇ v [ σ ]
                        → a ≤ʷ u [ σ ] ⊎ a ≤ʷ v [ σ ]
  sublattice-join-prime H u v p =
    any-pair (pool-extend v (pool-extend u (pool-base H))
                          (inj₂ (there (here refl)) ∷ inj₂ (here refl) ∷ []) p)
    where
    any-pair : {P : LatTerm X → Type χ} {x y : LatTerm X} → Any P (x ∷ y ∷ []) → P x ⊎ P y
    any-pair (here q)          = inj₁ q
    any-pair (there (here q))  = inj₂ q
```

#### Sufficiency: Corollary 1.12

Suppose `σ` freely generates.  Each member `σ i` is prime over the members
(`member-join-prime`{.AgdaFunction}), since `σ i` below a join of members indexed
by `Y` puts `i` in `Y`.  So by Lemma 1.3 each member is join prime in the
sublattice (`join-prime`{.AgdaFunction}).  Meet primality
(`meet-prime`{.AgdaFunction}) is the same fact for the dual family
`dual ∘ σ`{.AgdaFunction}, whose join condition is the meet condition of `σ`
(`MeetCondition→JoinConditionᵈ`{.AgdaFunction}), read back through
`dual-[]`{.AgdaFunction} and the order reversal of
[Classical.Structures.Lattice.Free.Duality][].

```agda
module _ {I : Type ι} {X : Type χ} (σ : Sub I X) where
  MeetCondition→JoinConditionᵈ : MeetCondition σ → JoinCondition (dual ∘ σ)
  MeetCondition→JoinConditionᵈ mc {i} {[]}      ()
  MeetCondition→JoinConditionᵈ mc {i} {j ∷ js}  p =
    mc {i} {j ∷ js} (dual-reflects (subst (dual (σ i) ≤ʷ_) (sym dual-meet) p))
    where
    dual-meet : dual (⋀⁺ (σ j) (map σ js)) ≡ ⋁⁺ (dual (σ j)) (map (dual ∘ σ) js)
    dual-meet = trans (dual-⋀⁺ (σ j) (map σ js)) (cong (⋁⁺ (dual (σ j))) (sym (map-∘ js)))

  module Sufficiency (jc : JoinCondition σ) (mc : MeetCondition σ) where
    member-join-prime : (i : I) {Y : List I} → σ i ≤⋁ map σ Y → Any (λ j → σ i ≤ʷ σ j) Y
    member-join-prime i p = Any.map (λ { refl → ≤ʷ-refl }) (jc p)

    join-prime : (i : I) (u v : LatTerm I) → σ i ≤ʷ u [ σ ] ∨̇ v [ σ ]
               → σ i ≤ʷ u [ σ ] ⊎ σ i ≤ʷ v [ σ ]
    join-prime i = sublattice-join-prime σ (σ i) (member-join-prime i)

    meet-prime : (j : I) (u v : LatTerm I) → u [ σ ] ∧̇ v [ σ ] ≤ʷ σ j
               → u [ σ ] ≤ʷ σ j ⊎ v [ σ ] ≤ʷ σ j
    meet-prime j u v p =
      ⊎-map (back u) (back v) (sublattice-join-prime (dual ∘ σ) (dual (σ j)) Hᵈ (dual u) (dual v) pᵈ)
      where
      Hᵈ : ∀ {Y : List I} → dual (σ j) ≤⋁ map (dual ∘ σ) Y → Any (λ k → dual (σ j) ≤ʷ dual (σ k)) Y
      Hᵈ q = Any.map (λ { refl → ≤ʷ-refl }) (MeetCondition→JoinConditionᵈ mc q)

      pᵈ : dual (σ j) ≤ʷ dual u [ dual ∘ σ ] ∨̇ dual v [ dual ∘ σ ]
      pᵈ = subst (dual (σ j) ≤ʷ_) (cong₂ _∨̇_ (dual-[] u σ) (dual-[] v σ)) (dual-antitone p)

      back : (w : LatTerm I) → dual (σ j) ≤ʷ dual w [ dual ∘ σ ] → w [ σ ] ≤ʷ σ j
      back w q = dual-reflects (subst (dual (σ j) ≤ʷ_) (sym (dual-[] w σ)) q)
```

With the members prime in the sublattice, the images of the generators obey all
six rules of Whitman's procedure there, and the substitution reflects the order
(`[]-reflects`{.AgdaFunction}), by the same recursion as the procedure itself.  A
join on the left (rule 2) and a meet on the right (rule 3) split, since
substitution keeps the formal operations; two generators (rule 1) are compared by
the join condition for a single index; a generator below a join (rule 4) and a meet
below a generator (rule 5) are the primality of the members; and a meet below a
join (rule 6) is Whitman's condition in `FL(X)`, the inversion
`∧≤∨-inv`{.AgdaFunction}, which applies because the images are themselves a formal
meet and a formal join.

```agda
    []-reflects : (s t : LatTerm I) → s [ σ ] ≤ʷ t [ σ ] → s ≤ʷ t
    []-reflects (s₁ ∨̇ s₂)    t            p = ∨≤ ([]-reflects s₁ t (proj₁ (∨≤-inv p)))
                                                 ([]-reflects s₂ t (proj₂ (∨≤-inv p)))
    []-reflects (ℊ i)        (t₁ ∧̇ t₂)    p = ℊ≤∧ ([]-reflects (ℊ i) t₁ (proj₁ (≤∧-inv p)))
                                                  ([]-reflects (ℊ i) t₂ (proj₂ (≤∧-inv p)))
    []-reflects (s₁ ∧̇ s₂)    (t₁ ∧̇ t₂)    p = ∧≤∧ ([]-reflects (s₁ ∧̇ s₂) t₁ (proj₁ (≤∧-inv p)))
                                                  ([]-reflects (s₁ ∧̇ s₂) t₂ (proj₂ (≤∧-inv p)))
    []-reflects (ℊ i)        (ℊ j)        p = ℊ≤ℊ (single (jc {i} {j ∷ []} p))
      where
      single : i ∈ j ∷ [] → i ≡ j
      single (here e) = e
    []-reflects (ℊ i)        (t₁ ∨̇ t₂)    p =
      [ (λ q → ℊ≤∨ˡ ([]-reflects (ℊ i) t₁ q)) , (λ q → ℊ≤∨ʳ ([]-reflects (ℊ i) t₂ q)) ]′
      (join-prime i t₁ t₂ p)
    []-reflects (s₁ ∧̇ s₂)    (ℊ j)        p =
      [ (λ q → ∧ˡ≤ℊ ([]-reflects s₁ (ℊ j) q)) , (λ q → ∧ʳ≤ℊ ([]-reflects s₂ (ℊ j) q)) ]′
      (meet-prime j s₁ s₂ p)
    []-reflects (s₁ ∧̇ s₂)    (t₁ ∨̇ t₂)    p =
      [ [ (λ q → ∧ˡ≤∨ ([]-reflects s₁ (t₁ ∨̇ t₂) q)) , (λ q → ∧ʳ≤∨ ([]-reflects s₂ (t₁ ∨̇ t₂) q)) ]′
      , [ (λ q → ∧≤∨ˡ ([]-reflects (s₁ ∧̇ s₂) t₁ q)) , (λ q → ∧≤∨ʳ ([]-reflects (s₁ ∧̇ s₂) t₂ q)) ]′
      ]′ (∧≤∨-inv p)
```

So a freely generating family induces an injective homomorphism
(`freelyGenerates→injective`{.AgdaFunction}), and `FL(I)` is a sublattice of
`FL(X)` by it (`freelyGenerates→≤`{.AgdaFunction}), in the sense of
[Setoid.Subalgebras.Basic][]: an injective homomorphism, whose image is the
sublattice the family generates.

```agda
  freelyGenerates→injective : FreelyGenerates σ → IsInjective (proj₁ (subst-hom σ))
  freelyGenerates→injective (jc , mc) = reflects→injective σ ([]-reflects _ _)
    where open Sufficiency jc mc

  freelyGenerates→≤ : FreelyGenerates σ → proj₁ (FL I) IsSubalgebraOf proj₁ (FL X)
  freelyGenerates→≤ fg = subst-hom σ , freelyGenerates→injective fg
```

#### Necessity

Conversely, if the induced homomorphism is injective, `σ` freely generates
(`injective→freelyGenerates`{.AgdaFunction}).  If `σ i` lies below the join of the
members indexed by `j ∷ js`, that join is the image of the join of the generators
`ℊ j`, `ℊ k` for `k` in `js` (`⋁⁺-ℊ-[]`{.AgdaFunction}), so by injectivity `ℊ i`
lies below the join of those generators in `FL(I)`; a generator is join prime there
(rule 4), and two generators are comparable only when equal (rule 1), so `i` is in
the list.  The meet condition is dual, with rule 5.  With sufficiency this is
Corollary 1.13 (`freelyGenerates⇔injective`{.AgdaFunction}).

```agda
module _ {I : Type ι} {X : Type χ} (σ : Sub I X) where
  ⋁⁺-ℊ-[] : (j : I) (js : List I) → ⋁⁺ (ℊ j) (map ℊ js) [ σ ] ≡ ⋁⁺ (σ j) (map σ js)
  ⋁⁺-ℊ-[] j js = trans (⋁⁺-[] σ (ℊ j) (map ℊ js)) (cong (⋁⁺ (σ j)) (sym (map-∘ js)))

  ⋀⁺-ℊ-[] : (j : I) (js : List I) → ⋀⁺ (ℊ j) (map ℊ js) [ σ ] ≡ ⋀⁺ (σ j) (map σ js)
  ⋀⁺-ℊ-[] j js = trans (⋀⁺-[] σ (ℊ j) (map ℊ js)) (cong (⋀⁺ (σ j)) (sym (map-∘ js)))

  module _ (inj : IsInjective (proj₁ (subst-hom σ))) where
    injective→JoinCondition : JoinCondition σ
    injective→JoinCondition {i} {[]}      ()
    injective→JoinCondition {i} {j ∷ js}  p =
      Any.map ℊ≤ℊ-inv (Any.map⁻ {f = ℊ} {xs = j ∷ js} (gen-⋁⁺-prime
        (injective→reflects σ {s = ℊ i} {t = ⋁⁺ (ℊ j) (map ℊ js)} inj
                            (subst (σ i ≤ʷ_) (sym (⋁⁺-ℊ-[] j js)) p))))

    injective→MeetCondition : MeetCondition σ
    injective→MeetCondition {i} {[]}      ()
    injective→MeetCondition {i} {j ∷ js}  p =
      Any.map (sym ∘ ℊ≤ℊ-inv) (Any.map⁻ {f = ℊ} {xs = j ∷ js} (⋀⁺-gen-below
        (injective→reflects σ {s = ⋀⁺ (ℊ j) (map ℊ js)} {t = ℊ i} inj
                            (subst (_≤ʷ σ i) (sym (⋀⁺-ℊ-[] j js)) p))))

    injective→freelyGenerates : FreelyGenerates σ
    injective→freelyGenerates = injective→JoinCondition , injective→MeetCondition

  freelyGenerates⇔injective : FreelyGenerates σ ⇔ IsInjective (proj₁ (subst-hom σ))
  freelyGenerates⇔injective = mk⇔ (freelyGenerates→injective σ) injective→freelyGenerates
```

Free generation depends only on the elements of the family, not on the terms
that name them: if `σ i` and `τ i` are equal in `FL(X)` for every `i`, and `σ`
freely generates, so does `τ` (`freelyGenerates-resp`{.AgdaFunction}).  The
substitutions agree on every term up to equality (`[]-congʳ`{.AgdaFunction}), so
injectivity passes from one to the other.  This lets a computation decide free
generation for a family of small terms and conclude it for equal but larger ones.

```agda
freelyGenerates-resp : {I : Type ι} {X : Type χ} {σ τ : Sub I X} → (∀ i → σ i ≈ʷ τ i)
                     → FreelyGenerates σ → FreelyGenerates τ
freelyGenerates-resp {σ = σ} {τ} e fg = injective→freelyGenerates τ λ {s} {t} p →
  freelyGenerates→injective σ fg
    (≈ʷ-trans ([]-congʳ s e) (≈ʷ-trans p (≈ʷ-sym ([]-congʳ t e))))
```

#### Deciding free generation

For an index set with decidable equality and a list `enum` of all its members, the
condition is decided by one decision of Whitman's procedure for each index and each
of the two halves (the module `Decide`{.AgdaModule}).  The members indexed by a
list not containing `i` all occur among the *others*, the indices different from
`i` (`others`{.AgdaFunction}), and the join over a sublist lies below the join over
the whole; so the join condition for `i` holds for every list exactly when `σ i`
does not lie below the join of the others
(`others-join`{.AgdaFunction}).  When `I` has a single element, the list of others
is empty and the condition holds outright.

```agda
module Decide {I : Type ι} {X : Type χ} (_≟ᴵ_ : DecidableEquality I) (enum : List I)
              (complete : ∀ i → i ∈ enum) (_≟_ : DecidableEquality X) where
  open Decision _≟_                using ( _≤ʷ?_ )
  open DecMembership _≟ᴵ_          using ( _∈?_ )

  others : I → List I
  others i = filter (λ j → ¬? (i ≟ᴵ j)) enum

  others-∌ : (i : I) → ¬ (i ∈ others i)
  others-∌ i i∈ = proj₂ (∈-filter⁻ (λ j → ¬? (i ≟ᴵ j)) {xs = enum} i∈) refl

  others-∋ : {i j : I} → ¬ (i ≡ j) → j ∈ others i
  others-∋ {i} {j} i≢j = ∈-filter⁺ (λ k → ¬? (i ≟ᴵ k)) (complete j) i≢j

  _≤⋁?_ : (b : LatTerm X) (G : List (LatTerm X)) → Dec (b ≤⋁ G)
  b ≤⋁? []        = no λ ()
  b ≤⋁? (g ∷ gs)  = b ≤ʷ? ⋁⁺ g gs

  _⋀≤?_ : (G : List (LatTerm X)) (b : LatTerm X) → Dec (G ⋀≤ b)
  [] ⋀≤? b        = no λ ()
  (g ∷ gs) ⋀≤? b  = ⋀⁺ g gs ≤ʷ? b
```

If `i` is not in `Y`, every member indexed by `Y` is one of the others
(`others-cover`{.AgdaFunction}), so its join lies below the join of the others
and its meet above their meet.

```agda
  module _ (σ : Sub I X) where
    others-cover : {i : I} {Y : List I} → ¬ (i ∈ Y) → ∀ {f} → f ∈ map σ Y → f ∈ map σ (others i)
    others-cover {i} {Y} i∉ f∈ =
      let (j , j∈ , f≡) = ∈-map⁻ σ f∈
      in subst (_∈ map σ (others i)) (sym f≡)
               (∈-map⁺ σ (others-∋ (λ i≡j → i∉ (subst (_∈ Y) (sym i≡j) j∈))))

    others-join : JoinCondition σ ⇔ All (λ i → ¬ (σ i ≤⋁ map σ (others i))) enum
    others-join = mk⇔ to from
      where
      to : JoinCondition σ → All (λ i → ¬ (σ i ≤⋁ map σ (others i))) enum
      to jc = tabulate λ {i} _ p → others-∌ i (jc p)

      from : All (λ i → ¬ (σ i ≤⋁ map σ (others i))) enum → JoinCondition σ
      from no-join {i} {Y} p = decidable-stable (i ∈? Y) λ i∉ →
        lookup no-join (complete i) (≤⋁-cover p λ f∈ → ∈→≤⋁ (others-cover i∉ f∈))

    others-meet : MeetCondition σ ⇔ All (λ i → ¬ (map σ (others i) ⋀≤ σ i)) enum
    others-meet = mk⇔ to from
      where
      to : MeetCondition σ → All (λ i → ¬ (map σ (others i) ⋀≤ σ i)) enum
      to mc = tabulate λ {i} _ p → others-∌ i (mc p)

      from : All (λ i → ¬ (map σ (others i) ⋀≤ σ i)) enum → MeetCondition σ
      from no-meet {i} {Y} p = decidable-stable (i ∈? Y) λ i∉ →
        lookup no-meet (complete i) (⋀≤-cover p λ f∈ → ∈→⋀≤ (others-cover i∉ f∈))

    joinCondition? : Dec (JoinCondition σ)
    joinCondition? = map′ (Equivalence.from others-join) (Equivalence.to others-join)
                          (all? (λ i → ¬? (σ i ≤⋁? map σ (others i))) enum)

    meetCondition? : Dec (MeetCondition σ)
    meetCondition? = map′ (Equivalence.from others-meet) (Equivalence.to others-meet)
                          (all? (λ i → ¬? (map σ (others i) ⋀≤? σ i)) enum)

    freelyGenerates? : Dec (FreelyGenerates σ)
    freelyGenerates? = joinCondition? ×-dec meetCondition?
```

#### Corollary 1.6: free generators are irreducible

A free generator `ℊ i` of `FL(I)` lies in the sublattice generated by a family
`τ` only if it equals a member of the family (`generator-in-image`{.AgdaFunction}):
if `ℊ i` equals the image `t [ τ ]` of a term, then by induction on `t`, a join
`u ∨ v` puts the generator below `u` or `v` (rule 4) and both above it (rule 2), so
it equals the image of `u` or of `v`; a meet is dual (rules 3 and 5); and a
generator `ℊ j` of the term gives the member `τ j`.  This is [Freese, Ježek, and
Nation (1995)][], Corollary 1.6(1), for free lattices: a generating set contains
the free generators.

Through an injective substitution `σ` the statement moves to the image: the
member `σ i` lies in the sublattice generated by a family expressed in the members,
`ρ ⨟ σ` for a family `ρ` in `FL(I)`, only if `ℊ i` equals a member of `ρ`
(`free-generator-in-image`{.AgdaFunction}).  So two freely generated sublattices
whose generators are related by `ρ` are equal only if each `ℊ i` is a member of
`ρ`; otherwise the containment is proper.

```agda
generator-in-image : {I : Type ι} {J : Type κ} (τ : Sub J I) (t : LatTerm J) {i : I}
                   → ℊ i ≈ʷ t [ τ ] → Σ[ j ∈ J ] ℊ i ≈ʷ τ j
generator-in-image τ (ℊ j)    e        = j , e
generator-in-image τ (u ∨̇ v)  (p , q)  =
  [ (λ p' → generator-in-image τ u (p' , proj₁ (∨≤-inv q)))
  , (λ p' → generator-in-image τ v (p' , proj₂ (∨≤-inv q))) ]′ (ℊ≤∨-inv p)
generator-in-image τ (u ∧̇ v)  (p , q)  =
  [ (λ q' → generator-in-image τ u (proj₁ (≤∧-inv p) , q'))
  , (λ q' → generator-in-image τ v (proj₂ (≤∧-inv p) , q')) ]′ (∧≤ℊ-inv q)

free-generator-in-image : {I : Type ι} {J : Type κ} {X : Type χ} (σ : Sub I X)
                        → IsInjective (proj₁ (subst-hom σ)) → (ρ : Sub J I) (t : LatTerm J) {i : I}
                        → σ i ≈ʷ t [ ρ ⨟ σ ] → Σ[ j ∈ J ] ℊ i ≈ʷ ρ j
free-generator-in-image σ inj ρ t {i} e =
  generator-in-image ρ t (inj {ℊ i} {t [ ρ ]} (subst (σ i ≈ʷ_) ([]-⨟ t ρ σ) e))
```
